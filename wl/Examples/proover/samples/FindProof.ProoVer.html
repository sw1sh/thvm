FindProof 0.1

Nik Murzin
Wolfram Institute, USA

Overview

FindProof 0.1 is a checker for TSTP first-order proofs, entered in ProoVer 2026. Checking is treated as proof replay: a proof is parsed into its sequence of annotated formulae, and each derived step is validated independently by dispatching on the inference rule that introduced it. A single SZS verdict is then reported for the whole proof, one of Verified, FailedVerified, or NotVerified. The checker is deliberately conservative. A step whose validity cannot be established is reported as NotVerified rather than guessed, so that an unsound proof is never accepted; soundness of the checker is favoured over coverage, in keeping with the scoring, which penalises accepting a buggy proof far more heavily than abstaining.

Implementation

The checker is implemented in the Wolfram Language. Formulae are parsed from the TSTP source by the WolframParser paclet, which yields symbolic expressions on which the per-rule obligations are discharged. For an instantiate step the conclusion is required to be a substitution instance of a parent, checked by pattern matching. For a negated_conjecture step the conclusion is required to be the logical negation of the parent: the negation of the parent is re-parsed through the same front end and compared with the stated conclusion up to variable renaming and negation-normal form, so that a wrong quantifier dual is caught. For a skolemize step a genuinely fresh Skolem symbol must be introduced and must actually occur in the resulting formula, a structural and equisatisfiability check, since Skolemisation is not a logical consequence. For a consequence, horn, or deduction step the conclusion is required to be entailed by the parents, which is decided by bounded Herbrand instantiation of the universally quantified premises followed by a propositional tautology test over the resulting ground atoms. The refutation is required to close with the empty clause, $false. Steps that use inference rules outside this repertoire cause the proof to be reported as NotVerified. The checker is run as a single command per proof through the Wolfram Engine.

Expected Competition Performance

On the provided examples the checker classifies every proof correctly, the three valid proofs as Verified and the four buggy proofs as FailedVerified, and in each buggy case it localises the faulty step: a universal conjecture negated as a universal rather than an existential, a single disjunct drawn from a disjunction, the reuse of an already-introduced Skolem symbol, and a declared Skolem symbol that is absent from the resulting formula. On the hidden set the checker is expected to be sound by construction, never accepting an unsound proof, and to abstain with NotVerified on proofs that use inference rules outside the implemented repertoire.
